Keeping plugins up to date

plugin-registry.md 12.7, for merlin v0.2.0; no API change. Automatic updates, Keep at This Version, removing a plugin's settings, Replaced by, and the update card's warning about plugins. Drawn as parts of Settings › Plugins (PluginBrowse.dc.html) and the update card (AppUpdateStates.html). All new; all need approval.
Automatic updates

1 · The setting

At the top of Settings › Plugins › Installed, and in settings.toml. User only, like Check for updates.

Update plugins automatically
Install plugin updates when they come out. An update that asks for more permissions waits for you.
plugins.auto_update

Uninstalling and replacing

4 · Removing its settings too

Off by default, so reinstalling gets them back. The card says what would go.

Uninstall Python?

Also delete its settings and data
3 settings in your settings.toml, 1 in this workspace's, and 1.2 MB it stored.
CancelUninstall

Settings in other workspaces aren't touched: merlin only knows this one's.

6 · Replaced by, when it's installed

A banner on its page and a chip in Installed. It keeps working; nothing changes until you choose.

Pyright is replaced by Python, which does what it did and more.
Keep PyrightSwitch

Switch installs Python, asking for its permissions as usual, then uninstalls Pyright (card 4).
